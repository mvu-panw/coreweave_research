The DPU is already spoken for

  • Every CKS node has a BlueField-3 DPU running Nimbus. It handles PXE bootstrapping, tenant VPC membership, L4/L7 policy, firewalling, encryption and VXLAN termination. Confirmed docs
  • GB300 Spectrum-X nodes pair BlueField-3 with ConnectX-8 SuperNICs, and Vera Rubin moves to ConnectX-9. CoreWeave has publicly committed to BlueField-4 (800 Gb/s, 6× the compute), built on DOCA, for "hardware-enforced tenant isolation". Confirmed BlueField-4
  • For PANW: Prisma AIRS on BlueField is designed for roughly 1–5 Gbps per DPU (inference endpoints, not training). At CoreWeave it is a platform roadmap conversation with the Nimbus team, not a near-term SKU, and Cisco is having the same conversation. Inferred

Fabric choice is shifting toward Ethernet

  • The Vera Rubin fabric runs on NVIDIA Spectrum-X SN6600-LD liquid-cooled switches (64×1.6T ports, 16 per rack = 1.64 Pb/s), with a 1:1 non-blocking leaf-to-spine design. Confirmed blog multi-plane
  • CoreWeave offers both Quantum InfiniBand (Quantum-2 and Quantum-X800, SHARP-compliant) and Spectrum-X RoCE. GB300 comes in either flavor, and Vera Rubin launches on Spectrum-X only. Confirmed docs
  • An Ethernet GPU fabric is still a back-end RDMA fabric. The same rule applies: no inline NGFW. Expect Cisco to pitch Nexus and Silicon One for front-end and storage networks. That is a switching sale, not a security one.
  • NVL72 racks are sold only as full racks of 18 nodes. At 2×200GbE per node, that is up to 7.2 Tb/s of north-south capacity per rack, which aggregates into the AZ edge that the firewalls size against.

Firewall sizing math (north-south only)

  • Per AZ edge: the limit is uplink and peering capacity (N×400G Direct Connect plus internet transit), not GPU count. Ask for the AZ's edge port count and the 95th-percentile egress. A 1.6–3.2 Tb/s edge needs threat-prevention-on throughput at PA-7500 class. Inferred
  • Per dedicated block: large customers (OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft) bring their own security inside the tenancy, but the block's internet and interconnect edge is CoreWeave's. A PA-5580 HA pair (300 Gbps threat prevention) per block keeps blast radius and logs separated.
  • Zone 0 / management: BMC, DPU (Nimbus) and switch management traffic is low-bandwidth but very high-consequence. PA-3440 per hall.
