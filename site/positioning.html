Firewall Positioning

Compete with Cisco on the network, complement CrowdStrike in the SOC, coexist with Teleport on access. Nothing goes in the GPU fabric.

Incumbents: Cisco, CrowdStrike, Teleport Account team. CrowdStrike is built into the CKS product and is a two-way commercial partner, so treat it as off-limits for displacement. Teleport is the engineers' access plane. Cisco is the one we're actually competing with, and the openings are where CoreWeave is still writing standards: OT (a new standards role is hiring now), the perimeter of each new campus, and the Zone 0 management plane. Why not the alternatives

Replacing Nimbus or the DPU firewall

CoreWeave built tenant isolation in-house on BlueField-3: Nimbus, EVPN Type-5, Cilium. It is the core of how they sell bare metal "without the need for virtual machines". Pitching an NGFW instead of it reads as not understanding their platform. Prisma AIRS on BlueField (roughly 1–5 Gbps per DPU) is a joint roadmap conversation for Vera Rubin and BlueField-4. Get there before Cisco's Hybrid Mesh Firewall-on-DPU pitch lands.

Anything in the GPU fabric

CoreWeave runs non-blocking Quantum-X800 InfiniBand and Spectrum-X RoCE with SHARP and GPUDirect RDMA at 3.2–6.4 Tb/s per node. Nothing inline belongs there, whether it is InfiniBand or Ethernet. Say this first, unprompted. It shows we understand AI infrastructure and keeps the conversation on north-south, where we win.

Talk track

"Secure the AI factory around the fabric, not in it"

  1. "We never touch your GPU fabric or Nimbus." Every enforcement point is north-south, Zone 0, or OT. Tenant isolation stays exactly as you designed it.
  2. "Valvey can shut down a rack, so who can reach Valvey?" Software-defined cooling and power control is the highest-consequence system in the building. Segment it, baseline it, and log every command.
  3. "Your OT standard is being written now, so write it once." One OT and IoT visibility plus segmentation standard across 20+ sites and several landlords, managed from one console, with remote vendor access through Prisma Access instead of ad hoc VPNs.
  4. "Threat prevention on, at 400G." PA-7500 and PA-5500 sized on threat-on throughput at the AZ edge. Ask Cisco for the same number with IPS, URL and DNS security enabled.
  5. "Keep Falcon. Stop building a SIEM." XSIAM ingests CrowdStrike natively and adds network, OT and DPU telemetry. Your detection engineers write detections instead of running data pipelines.
  6. "Keep Teleport. Cover everyone it doesn't." Contractors, DC technicians and OT vendors get ZTNA 2.0 and Prisma Access Browser. Zone 0 firewalls make Teleport sessions network-constrained as well as identity-constrained.
  7. "Sell security, don't just buy it." CrowdStrike is in your Partner Network. CN-Series and Prisma AIRS give your regulated and federal tenants L7 and AI-runtime security that Nimbus doesn't provide.

Objection handling

  • "Cisco is an investor and our network vendor." Cisco IOS in technician postings means switching and routing, not a security standard. Security is a separate decision with a separate owner (the CISO organization). Investment doesn't require standardizing on a vendor, and the OT and Zone 0 decisions are still open.
  • "Cisco's firewall runs on BlueField now." On a DPU that Nimbus already owns? Ask who would run it and how it would coexist with Nimbus firewalling and VXLAN termination. A DPU firewall also doesn't cover OT, the campus edge or field access.
  • "Firewalls slow down training." Agreed, which is why none go in the training path. We size only the internet, peering and interconnect edge, at line rate with threat prevention on.
  • "CrowdStrike already does this." Falcon protects hosts and cloud workloads. It doesn't segment a BMS network, inspect 400G of edge traffic, or give a contractor least-privilege access. We integrate with Falcon. We don't replace it.
  • "Teleport is our zero trust." For engineers, yes. Zero trust for the network (Zone 0 segmentation), for OT vendors and for unmanaged contractors is a different control.
  • "The landlord owns OT at colo sites." CoreWeave owns the liability when a facility event takes down a customer AZ. Push the OT standard into colo contracts and monitor the boundary.
  • "Federal needs something different." CoreWeave Federal is pursuing FedRAMP and hiring for NIPR/SIPR, STIG and FIPS work. PANW brings FedRAMP High authorized Prisma Access and FIPS-mode PAN-OS. Bring in the PANW federal team.
