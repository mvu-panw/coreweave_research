LIVINGSTON, N.J., March 24, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- CoreWeave, the AI Hyperscaler™, today announced it has appointed Jean English as Chief Marketing Officer. With over a decade of CMO experience at several cloud, AI, and cybersecurity leaders, English has established a track record of executing data-driven, multi-channel marketing and communications campaigns supporting sustainable sales and product growth in the U.S. and abroad.
"CoreWeave is helping to power the AI innovations reshaping our lives. At this pivotal moment, Jean will be instrumental in further progressing our market presence and leadership," said Michael Intrator, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer at CoreWeave. "As a new member of CoreWeave's leadership team, Jean's extensive marketing and communications expertise will be integral to stewarding and working to grow our brand."
"CoreWeave has established itself as a leading provider of highly-performant, efficient, and scalable AI cloud solutions," said Jean English, Chief Marketing Officer at CoreWeave. "I'm excited to help shape the company's next chapter and further elevate our brand and leadership position in the market."
English brings over 20 years of marketing and communications leadership, with a decade as CMO at leading cloud, AI, and cybersecurity companies. Most recently, she was CMO at Juniper Networks, known for its AI-native networking platform, where she focused on driving global growth in the enterprise segment. English has also served as CMO at Palo Alto Networks, a global leader in cybersecurity; NetApp, a pioneer in cloud data services and storage; and IBM's Asia Pacific division, where she led initiatives to accelerate growth in cloud and cognitive computing. At IBM, she spent over 10 years in marketing leadership, ultimately serving as VP of Global Marketing for IBM Cloud, where she helped to drive the transformation of its multi-billion-dollar Cloud as a Service business.
English is the latest addition to CoreWeave's growing leadership team, building on several other strategic hires this year, including Chief Information Security Officer Jim Higgins, who most recently served as the CISO for Snap Inc. and Block, Inc. and Chief Information Officer Sandy Venugopal, who has served as Chief Information Officer for both SentinelOne and Uber.About CoreWeave
CoreWeave is The Essential Cloud for AI™️. Built for pioneers by pioneers, CoreWeave delivers a fully integrated platform of technology and teams that enables innovators to move at the pace of innovation, building and scaling AI with confidence. Trusted by 9 of the 10 leading foundation model providers, CoreWeave serves as a force multiplier by combining superior infrastructure performance with deep technical expertise to accelerate human discovery. Established in 2017, CoreWeave completed its public listing on Nasdaq (CRWV) in March 2025.
