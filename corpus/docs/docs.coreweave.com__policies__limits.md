# Limits

> Physical limits and customer quotas for CoreWeave Kubernetes Service

CoreWeave Kubernetes Service (CKS) manages physical limits and customer quotas to ensure the stability and performance of the platform. CoreWeave limits the number of clusters, instances, and storage you can provision. To learn about your account's limits and request an upgrade, [please submit a support ticket](https://docs.coreweave.com/support.md).

## Physical limits

CoreWeave Kubernetes Service (CKS) has certain physical limits that are enforced to ensure the stability and performance of the platform. These limits are based on the underlying infrastructure, and are subject to change based on the availability of resources.

## Customer quotas

CoreWeave Kubernetes Service (CKS) enforces customer quotas to ensure fair usage of resources and to prevent abuse. These quotas are set based on the customer's subscription plan and can be adjusted upon request.
