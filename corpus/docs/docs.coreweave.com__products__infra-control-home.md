# Infra Control

> Overview of CoreWeave Kubernetes Service (CKS) and the CoreWeave storage and networking products

# Clusters, storage, and networking for AI at scale

Infra Control brings together CoreWeave Kubernetes Service (CKS) and the storage and networking products your clusters use, from S3-compatible object storage to InfiniBand interconnect.

Choose a product to get started.

## Kubernetes

CoreWeave Kubernetes Service runs managed Kubernetes clusters on bare-metal GPU and CPU Nodes.

[CoreWeave Kubernetes Service
Managed Kubernetes on bare metal, purpose-built for training, inference, and HPC at scale.
Explore CKS](https://docs.coreweave.com/products/cks.md)
