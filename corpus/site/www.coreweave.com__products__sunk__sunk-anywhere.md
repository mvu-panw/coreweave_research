### More of the training environment in one system

SUNK integrates more of the training environment into one system, helping teams avoid the fragmented tooling and manual coordination that often emerge in external environments.

### Hybrid scheduling and observability hooks

SUNK integrates hybrid scheduling, researcher-facing capabilities, and observability hooks, giving teams stronger visibility and control as workloads scale.

### Faster path to productive training

SUNK helps teams get to a working production cluster faster, with built-in primitives for access, user environments, and containers. Teams spend less time on setup and more time on productive training work.

### One way of operating across environments

SUNK Anywhere extends SUNK beyond CoreWeave rather than introducing a separate external training stack. Teams preserve familiar interfaces, scheduling behavior, and day-to-day operating practices as infrastructure footprints grow.
