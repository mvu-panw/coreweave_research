### Your day-to-day doesn't change

Your account, projects, teams, and API keys stay the same. As of September 30, 2026, Free, Pro, and Enterprise SaaS accounts now sign in through CoreWeave Forge with the credentials you already use. Self-hosted and dedicated-instance accounts remain unchanged.

EXISTING COREWEAVE CUSTOMER

### What the acquisition opens up for CoreWeave customers

CoreWeave Forge brings Weights & Biases experiment tracking and evaluation together with Training, Inference, Registry, and CoreWeave Sandboxes. Your teams can build, train, evaluate, and run models and agents in one connected environment, next to the infrastructure you already run on.

### Powerful separately, better together

Weights & Biases brings the tools AI teams use to track, evaluate, and improve models. CoreWeave brings the AI cloud built for the most demanding workloads. Together in CoreWeave Forge, you get one place to run the whole AI loop. Weights & Biases stays cloud-agnostic so you can work wherever your workloads live.

### Proven at scale

More than 1 million AI practitioners at 1,400+ companies use Weights & Biases, and teams have tracked more than 1 billion runs on the platform.

### One connected loop

CoreWeave Forge connects Weights & Biases with Training, Inference, Registry, and Sandboxes, so teams move from experiment to production without switching tools.

### Any model, framework, or cloud

Weights & Biases stays cloud-agnostic, so you keep working wherever your workloads run.
