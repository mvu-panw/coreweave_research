# Dedicated VAST storage
For hybrid and specialized workloads that need access to VAST’s ecosystem. Get direct VAST Management System (VMS) access, physical isolation with dedicated hardware, and the full VAST platform, including DataBase, DataEngine, snapshots, replication, and more. Your data, your cluster.

Contact sales

### VAST global access and replication

Interoperate with other VAST clusters. Enable cross-cluster replication and Global Namespace access.

### Unique encryption keys

Per-tenant encryption keys with encryption at rest enabled on cluster launch.

### Detailed audit logging

Full control over VAST auditing and logging services for compliance and visibility.

### QoS and quota controls

Per-tenant quality-of-service and quota management via direct VMS access.

### SSO/SAML identity provider support

VAST integrates with your identity provider for centralized access control and single sign-on.

### Physical isolation

Dedicated hardware with complete physical storage separation from other CoreWeave customers.

### Full VMS access

Direct VAST Management System access with failover. Per-tenant controls including QoS and quota management.

### Protocol flexibility

Connect via NFS, S3, or NVMe-over-TCP using any VAST-supported protocol. PVC support via standard VAST CSI driver.

### Latest VAST features

Access DataBase, DataEngine, snapshots, audit logging, the VAST Catalog, and new VAST capabilities as they ship.
