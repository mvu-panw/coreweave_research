Transcript

CoreWeave Mission Control™ is the operating standard for running AI on CoreWeave Cloud, an approach to designing and operating infrastructure that consistently delivers reliability, transparency, and insight at scale. Mission Control unifies security, talent services, and observability to reduce disruptions, surface performance issues early, and feed rich telemetry into your existing tools. Automated node and fleet lifecycle management, Telemetry Relay, GPU Straggler Detection (Preview), and the Mission Control Agent (Preview) all work as part of a single, coherent system rather than standalone features. Whether you’re assessing platform readiness or responsible for training and serving models in production, this quick video demonstrates what the operating standard for AI looks like in practice—and how Mission Control helps teams see more, act faster, and run with confidence.
