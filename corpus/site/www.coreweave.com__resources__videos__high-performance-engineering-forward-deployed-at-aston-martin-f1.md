Transcript

Formula 1 is a technological arms race, with results laid bare 24 times a season on the track. There is nowhere to hide. In that context, CoreWeave's forward deployed engineers sit inside Aston Martin Aramco F1 Team, working alongside the team's own engineers. In this session you will hear from our CoreWeave engineers on what it takes to operate inside a Formula 1 team, walking through three workstreams and detailing the problem, what was built and what it changed. Learnings and models which can be applied to any team moving AI from experiment to production against immovable deadlines.
Speakers: Eric Ernst, Commercial Technology Ambassador, Aston Martin Aramco Formula One; Sara Sathyanandha, Forward Deployed AI Engineer, CoreWeave; Morgan McGuire, Senior Manager, AI Engineering, CoreWeave; Agata Młynarczyk, AI Engineer, CoreWeave
