## Episode 1: AI Cloud Essentials Podcast
AI budgets are under pressure, and the hidden “hyperscaler tax” is often to blame. In this episode, host Ritu Jyoti breaks down how general-purpose clouds create hidden fees, wasted GPU cycles, and forecasting blind spots that quietly erode AI ROI.
Ritu outlines how AI-native cloud architecture improves utilization, eliminates surprise costs, and delivers predictable pricing aligned to real business outcomes. If rising cloud bills are slowing your AI strategy, this episode offers a clearer path to controlling spend and unlocking measurable ROI.
