# Mike Intrator

Chief Executive Officer, Co-founder

Chief Executive Officer, Co-founder

Copied

Michael Intrator is co-founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of CoreWeave, the Essential Cloud for AI. Since 2017, he has led CoreWeave from startup to publicly traded company at the center of global AI adoption. Today, its purpose-built cloud powers many of the world’s most advanced AI models and production workloads, including nine of the 10 leading foundation model providers.
Intrator recognized early that AI would require more than access to computing capacity and built CoreWeave around a thesis that combines technology, infrastructure and capital on a platform specifically designed for the AI era.
Before CoreWeave, Intrator co-founded Hudson Ridge Asset Management, a natural gas-focused hedge fund, and held several roles at Natsource Asset Management.
Intrator earned a B.A. from Binghamton University and an M.P.A. from Columbia University’s School of International and Public Affairs.
