## Episode 5: AI Cloud Essentials Podcast
AI cloud migration doesn’t have to be risky or disruptive. In this episode, Ritu Jyoti sits down with Corey Sanders, SVP of Strategy at CoreWeave, to debunk the biggest myths around moving to an AI-native cloud and explain why it’s less about a lift-and-shift and more about replatforming for intelligence.
You’ll learn how general-purpose clouds create hidden costs and operational drag for AI workloads, and how incremental, zero-downtime migration strategies unlock faster experimentation and real ROI. If rising cloud bills and stalled AI initiatives are holding you back, this conversation outlines a clearer, lower-risk path forward.
