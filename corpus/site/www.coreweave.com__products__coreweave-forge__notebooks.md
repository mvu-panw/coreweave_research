### Seamless analysis of your project data

No need to export project data or rebuild experiment context in another tool. Your notebook starts connected to the experiment data already in your project, so you can stay focused on the question at hand.

### A reactive notebook experience

No more stale outputs or hidden state. CoreWeave Notebooks track dependencies between cells and rerun whatever a change affects, so what you see is always what your code produced.

### Leverage learnings from your team

Notebooks live inside a shared project, not in someone's local files. Browse what your team has already built, open it with the data attached, and copy it as a starting point for your own work.
