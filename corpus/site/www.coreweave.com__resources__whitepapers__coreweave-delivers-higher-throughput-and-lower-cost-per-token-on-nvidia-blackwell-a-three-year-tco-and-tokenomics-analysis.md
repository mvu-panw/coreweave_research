Choosing AI infrastructure takes more than comparing GPU hourly rates. Signal65’s analysis examines how CoreWeave’s infrastructure pricing and inference performance shape the cost of running AI on NVIDIA Blackwell. Download the report to compare three-year total cost of ownership, cost per million tokens, and serverless inference pricing against three hyperscaler clouds.
- Explore modeled three-year infrastructure savings of up to 65% on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72, including GPU, storage, networking, observability, and support costs.
- See how throughput and pricing combine to deliver up to 66% lower modeled cost per million tokens for DeepSeek-R1 on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72.
- Compare serverless inference pricing across 17 models, with CoreWeave delivering 26% lower median input token costs and 15% lower median output token costs.
