#### Get the evidence to know what will work, what to improve, and what it takes to reach production
Benchmarks show how infrastructure performs under ideal conditions. CoreWeave ARENA shows how your own workload performs under production-like load. In this brief, learn how to:
- Test training, inference, and RL workloads on production-grade infrastructure, including newer hardware that's often not yet generally available
- Measure performance and cost from your own run, tied to success criteria you set with a CoreWeave AI expert
- Use CoreWeave Forge and CoreWeave ARIA to understand why runs behave as they do and what to improve next
