## From a plan to inspectable results

Define driving scenarios across towns, weather, and traffic. Run them in parallel. Get back recordings and per-scenario results.
A finished job isn’t proof that every scenario ran, or that any of them passed. A CARLA campaign reports three things separately: whether execution completed, whether outputs came back for every scenario, and how the results scored against your driving criteria. Delivered separately, they tell your team exactly what to do next.
