Transcript

The US and Europe have long been dominant forces in global tech innovation. However, Europe is increasingly asserting its influence.
This on-demand panel discussion, recorded at London Tech Week 2025, covers:
- What strategic steps can be taken by European organisations to solidify their role on the world stage
- Current methods for growing startups and what support can be offered to accelerate R&D
- How Europe can strike the right balance between strong privacy regulation and create an environment conducive to innovation in tech development,
- Where should government investment be heading, particularly when it comes to workforce upskilling and tech infrastructure upgrades.
‍Moderator: Lizzy Burden, Anchor & UK Correspondent - Bloomberg TV
Panelists
- Mike Mattacola, CoreWeave Chief Business Officer
- Markus Villig, Founder & CEO - Bolt
- Suranga Chandratillake, General Partner - Balderton Capital
- Baroness Martha Lane-Fox of Soho CBE, British Entrepreneur, Public Servant & Digital Leader
