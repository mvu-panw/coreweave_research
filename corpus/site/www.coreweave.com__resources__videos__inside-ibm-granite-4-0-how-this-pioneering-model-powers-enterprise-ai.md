Join IBM’s Brian Belgodere and CoreWeave’s Navarre Pratt as they explore the trials and triumphs of training entirely on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 infrastructure—one of the first deployments of its kind. Discover what this breakthrough means for speed, scalability, and innovation for teams looking to accelerate their AI roadmaps.
- How IBM and CoreWeave built one of the first NVIDIA GB200 NVL72-powered AI supercomputers
- How IBM Granite 4.0 enables faster model development and open-source flexibility for enterprises
- Lessons from solving first-of-its-kind challenges in rack power density, cooling, and observability at scale
