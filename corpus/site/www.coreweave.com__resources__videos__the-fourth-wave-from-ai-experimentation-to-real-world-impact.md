In this episode of Who’s Ready for Anything, CoreWeave Chief Marketing Officer Jean English sits down with author and technologist Matt Watts to discuss his latest book, The Fourth Wave, and why AI, data, and infrastructure have become the foundation of competitive advantage.
Discover how pioneering organizations are moving beyond AI experimentation to create lasting business impact, why infrastructure has become a strategic differentiator, and what leaders must do today to prepare for the next era of AI-driven transformation.
In this session, you’ll learn:
- Why AI, data, and infrastructure now define competitive advantage
- How leading organizations are turning AI ambition into business outcomes
- What The Fourth Wave means for enterprise AI adoption
- Why infrastructure is becoming a strategic business decision
- How leaders can prepare for the next wave of AI innovation
