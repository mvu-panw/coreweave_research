# Jeff Baker

Chief Accounting Officer

Chief Accounting Officer

Copied

Jeff Baker serves as CoreWeave’s Chief Accounting Officer, a role he has held since August 2024. He brings over two decades of financial leadership experience across high-growth and Fortune 500 companies. Prior to joining CoreWeave, Mr. Baker was the Chief Accounting Officer at Rivian from May 2021 to July 2024. During his tenure, he played a pivotal role in the company’s initial public offering and led the development of scalable accounting infrastructure to support its rapid expansion. Mr. Baker holds a Bachelor of Science in Accountancy from the University of Florida and a Master of Accountancy from Stetson University.
