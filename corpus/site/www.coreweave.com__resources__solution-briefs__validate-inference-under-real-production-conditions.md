Benchmarks measure performance under ideal conditions. CoreWeave ARENA verifies performance under actual conditions. In this solution brief, you’ll learn how to structure a validation run for CoreWeave Inference in a real-world production environment, CoreWeave ARENA. 
Get a high-level overview that shows you how to:
- Test your own model and traffic patterns at production concurrency, not a reduced test scale
- Measure latency stability, availability scaling, and recovery under a real demand spike
- Map cost directly to GPU utilization for forecasting you can defend to executives and boards
