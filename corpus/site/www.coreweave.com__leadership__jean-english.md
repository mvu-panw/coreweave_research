Jean English is Chief Marketing Officer at CoreWeave, where she leads the company’s global marketing and communications strategy. She built CoreWeave's marketing function from the ground up, establishing The Essential Cloud for AI™ as the company's defining market position and helping shape how customers, partners, and the broader industry understand CoreWeave's role in the AI ecosystem.

English’s background includes elevating and differentiating brands across AI, cloud, and cybersecurity. Prior to CoreWeave, she served as CMO at Juniper Networks, and previously held CMO roles at Palo Alto Networks, NetApp, and IBM Asia Pacific.

She holds a B.S. in Business Administration from the University of North Carolina at Wilmington.
