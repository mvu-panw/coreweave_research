## NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 on CoreWeave Cloud: Built for Agentic AI
NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 packs 72 Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs into a single rack, built for pretraining, post-training, test-time scaling, and agentic scaling. Versus Blackwell: four times faster training, one-fourth the GPUs, 10 times higher inference throughput per watt, one-tenth the cost per million tokens. 100 radiology scans an hour on Blackwell becomes 1,000 on Vera Rubin, same power draw.
CoreWeave optimized every layer of the stack to unlock it, from CoreWeave Mission Control's liquid cooling and rack management to SUNK's topology-aware scheduling and CoreWeave ARENA's cost transparency and expert access.
