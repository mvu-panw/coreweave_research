## How modern infrastructure delivers reliability, isolation, and observability
For quant teams, security is no longer just about controlling access to infrastructure. As training and backtesting scale to hundreds or thousands of GPUs, security now depends on proving that workloads execute reliably, deterministically, and as intended. 
This whitepaper examines why legacy on-prem and general-purpose cloud models break down at scale, and outlines a new security standard built on reliable execution, enforced isolation, and continuous observability.
Inside the whitepaper:
- Why reliability and security become inseparable as quant workloads scale
- Where on-prem and traditional cloud models fail long-running, distributed research
- How purpose-built AI clouds enable auditable, reproducible execution at scale
