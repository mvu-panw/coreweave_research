Sachin Jain is Chief Operating Officer at CoreWeave, where he leads global operations including supply chain, procurement, capacity planning, IT engineering, data center operations, and international expansion. 
He plays a critical role in scaling the infrastructure and operational capabilities that enable CoreWeave to meet the rapidly growing demand for AI, overseeing the coordination required to deliver infrastructure capacity efficiently around the world.
Prior to CoreWeave, Jain held senior leadership roles at Oracle Cloud, Google Cloud and Amazon.
He holds a Bachelor of Technology in Manufacturing Science & Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, an M.S. in Computer Science, and an M.S. in Industrial Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
