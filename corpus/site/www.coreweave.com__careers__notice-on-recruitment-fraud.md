## What is recruitment fraud?
Recruitment fraud is a scheme in which fictitious job opportunities are presented to job seekers usually in an attempt to obtain personal information or money. This type of fraud is typically carried out through online services, such as fraudulent websites or through unsolicited emails or messages impersonating a legitimate company. These communications may request personal information or payment as part of an illegitimate recruiting process.

## CoreWeave recruitment guidelines
To help protect candidates, please note the following CoreWeave recruiting guidelines:
- CoreWeave recruiters will only contact you from verified company email addresses ending in @coreweave.com.
- We will never ask you to participate in interviews conducted solely over text message, WhatsApp, Telegram, or other informal messaging platforms.
- We will never request financial information, payments, gift cards, banking details, or sensitive personal data (such as Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, or account passwords) during the hiring process.
- We will never ask you to purchase equipment or make any upfront payments.
- Legitimate CoreWeave roles may be listed on trusted platforms such as LinkedIn; however, all genuine postings will ultimately direct candidates to our official Careers site. ‘
Additional Information
- CoreWeave is not responsible for any financial loss or damages resulting from recruitment fraud conducted by third parties.
- For your safety, we encourage you to verify the authenticity of communications by checking email domains, avoiding unsolicited attachments or links, and confirming that the roles ultimately route to our official careers page.
