#### Why lasting advantage starts with a purpose-built AI cloud
AI is expanding what teams can discover and build. A successful pilot still leaves the hard work of making AI reliable for real users, managing rising costs, and turning production failures into tested improvements. This paper examines what an AI cloud has to provide to turn production experience into lasting advantage, and why infrastructure and development must be considered together.
