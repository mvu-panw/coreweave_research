AI workloads move in waves. Your cloud should, too. CoreWeave Capacity Plans—Reservations, Flex Reservations (Preview), Spot (GA), and On-Demand—give teams a structured way to match cost and certainty to how AI actually runs.
In this solution brief, you’ll learn how to:
- Separate guaranteed access from 24/7 economics
- Protect critical peaks without overbuying baseline capacity
- Use Spot and On-Demand strategically for tolerant workloads
- Reduce waste driven by guesswork-based commitments
- Operate AI production environments with clarity and control

This portfolio approach to capacity planning for AI workloads sets a new standard for how AI clouds operate—so you can reserve what’s steady, flex what must be protected, and optimize what can move.
