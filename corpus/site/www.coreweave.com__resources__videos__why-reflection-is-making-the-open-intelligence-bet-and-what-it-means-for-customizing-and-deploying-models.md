From building PyTorch into the world's leading open-source AI framework to leading the Llama open-source initiative at Meta, Joe Spisak has spent his career at the center of the open AI movement. In this session, Joe and Lavanya Shukla - the former Head of Growth and VP of AI at Weights & Biases - will discuss Reflection's vision for open intelligence, the case for open foundation models, and why keeping that intelligence accessible to everyone may be the most consequential bet in AI today.
Speakers: Joe Spisak, VP of Product & Head of Open Source, Reflection; Lavanya Shukla, Managing Partner, Improbability Engine Capital
