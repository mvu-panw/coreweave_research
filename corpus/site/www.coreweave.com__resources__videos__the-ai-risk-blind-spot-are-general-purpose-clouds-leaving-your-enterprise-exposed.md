## Episode 4: AI Cloud Essentials Podcast
AI cloud security is no longer a future issue — it’s an immediate enterprise risk. In this episode, host Ritu Jyoti sits down with James Higgins, Chief Security Officer at CoreWeave, to examine why traditional perimeter defenses and legacy cloud security tools fail to protect modern AI workloads.
Together, they explore how AI reshapes the shared responsibility model and introduces new risks such as prompt injection, data poisoning, and model drift. You’ll learn why AI-native infrastructure, zero trust, and data-centric security are essential for protecting models, prompts, and proprietary data.
The conversation also outlines practical next steps enterprise leaders can take to strengthen governance, reduce exposure, and secure AI workloads before blind spots turn into breaches.
