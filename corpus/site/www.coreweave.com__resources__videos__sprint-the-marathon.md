### Episode 14: AI Cloud Essentials Podcast
In Episode 14 of the AI Cloud Essentials Podcast, Pim de Witte, CEO and Co-Founder of General Intuition, joins host Ritu Jyoti to explore how player-generated gaming data is becoming the foundation for a new generation of AI models. Learn how gameplay clips capture sequences of decisions and reasoning that can be used to train models for robotics and other physical AI applications.
Discover why infrastructure reliability is essential for high-velocity AI research, how purpose-built cloud infrastructure helps lean teams move faster, and what it takes to scale AI from virtual environments into the physical world.
In this session, you’ll learn:
- How gaming data can improve AI model pre-training
- Why consistent input spaces help models transfer to robotics
- How infrastructure reliability impacts research velocity
- Why GPU uptime and Kubernetes matter for lean AI teams
- What physical AI means for the next generation of AI innovation
