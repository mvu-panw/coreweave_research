At CoreWeave, our engineers are at the forefront of designing and building the next-generation AI HyperscalerTM stack. From the ground up, we power the creation and delivery of cutting-edge intelligence that fuels innovation across industries. We’re seeking engineers who are problem solvers and trailblazers—passionate about tackling complex challenges and working with the latest technologies.

You’ll have the opportunity to take ownership of your work, collaborate with top talent, and contribute directly to the success of our customers and your own personal growth. At CoreWeave, your impact will be felt, and your career will thrive.

### Healthcare

Our employees have access to high-quality medical, dental, and vision coverage.

### Carrot fertility and family

Employees have access to Carrot for support in their family-forming journey, from pre-pregnancy to menopause to gender-affirming care.

### Kinside childcare concierge service

Keep family a top priority. We provide white glove childcare consulting services through Kinside so you can be the best parent—while changing the world.

### Mental wellness

Manage life’s curveballs with a confidential and complimentary program. Our benefits include counseling, support, and resources through Spring Health for both our employees and their families. We also give access to Calm, a meditation app that helps you focus a few minutes on your well-being.

### Life and disability insurance

You deserve peace of mind for the unexpected. That’s why we provide our employees with top-tier life insurance and short-term and long-term disability insurance.

### Flexible spending accounts

Keep a rainy-day health fund. Our employees can contribute pre-tax dollars toward a medical and/or dependent care FSA. We also offer commuter FSA.

### 401(k) with generous match

Plan ahead for retirement with ease. At CoreWeave, we match up to 4% of your salary in a company 401(k) plan.

### Flexible PTO

We believe in a healthy work-life balance. Take the time you need to recharge with flexible PTO.

### Catered lunch

Come together and mangia. We provide catered lunches in office and at our data centers from various local vendors. Get a salad, a burger, even a boba if you want.

### Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

At CoreWeave, we believe in sharing success. Our Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) makes it simple to invest in CoreWeave’s future — and yours — by offering employees the opportunity to purchase shares at a 15% discount.

Expand

With our ESPP, employees can directly take part in CoreWeave’s growth.

#### How It Works
Employees can elect to contribute 1% to 15% of their base salary through automatic payroll deductions, with a $25,000 annual limit on share purchases, accumulating funds over a six-month offering period. At the end of the period, shares are purchased at a 15% discount off the lower of the stock price at the beginning or end of the offering period — helping maximize the value of their investment.
Our ESPP is designed to make ownership easy and accessible, reinforcing our commitment to building CoreWeave’s future together.
*ESPP ic currently only available for U.S.-based employees. Other locations are being evaluated for future roll-out.
