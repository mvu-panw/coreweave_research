# Max Hjelm

Executive Vice President, Strategic Deals

Executive Vice President, Strategic Deals

Copied

Max Hjelm is Executive Vice President of Strategic Deals at CoreWeave, where he is primarily responsible for the company’s key customer engagements. Since joining CoreWeave in 2020, Hjelm has been instrumental in building the Revenue organization from the ground up, helping establish CoreWeave as the AI cloud platform of choice for the world's leading AI innovators.
During his tenure, Hjelm built and scaled the Go-to-Market function through every stage of the company's growth, developing the customer base, team, and commercial strategy that underpins CoreWeave's position in the market today. His work spans landmark customer deals, revenue diversification, and the ongoing effort to bring CoreWeave's full-stack AI platform to a broader set of customers across industries.
Prior to CoreWeave, Hjelm held business development roles at AspireIQ and Curalate. He holds a B.S. from Haverford College.
