# Brannin McBee

Chief Development Officer, Co-founder

Chief Development Officer, Co-founder

Copied

Brannin McBee is co-founder and Chief Development Officer at CoreWeave, where he leads the company's business development, financing, and strategic partnerships. McBee spent CoreWeave’s first seven years as chief strategy officer, helping establish the company as the AI cloud of choice for leading AI innovators, before assuming his current role.

As a key architect of CoreWeave's growth strategy, McBee developed the innovative financing approaches that enabled the company to rapidly scale AI infrastructure and meet the accelerating demand for AI compute. His work also helped shape the partnerships and investment strategies behind CoreWeave's continued expansion.

Prior to CoreWeave, he served as a proprietary trader at Active Power Investments, where he focused on North American natural gas, power, and agriculture markets. He also held leadership roles at Fourth Floor Coastal LLC and Windy Bay Power LLC.

He holds a B.S. in Finance from the University of Colorado Boulder.
