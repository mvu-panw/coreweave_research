### Intelligent agent observability and improvement, built for enterprise scale
Production agents generate huge volumes of conversations and traces, which makes it difficult to see what users are asking and where agents fail. CoreWeave Agent Lens automatically analyzes your production traffic and surfaces recurring failures and capability gaps, so your team can work from a prioritized list of insights rather than raw logs.

#### What you'll see in the video
- Observe: go from tens of thousands of conversations to one dashboard showing volume, errors, cost, latency, and tool usage
- Detect: failures are grouped into categories like missed requirements, tool misuse, and lost context; each category shows its share of traffic, user frustration, and cost per turn
- Diagnose: open any trace and step through it turn by turn to find the exact tool call that failed
- Improve: build an LLM judge, check it against human labels, then test a fix candidate across hundreds of cases to catch regressions before you ship
