# James Higgins

Chief Information Security Officer

Chief Information Security Officer

Copied

Jim Higgins, CISO of CoreWeave, is a seasoned technology and business leader with over 30 years of experience in the high-technology sector, specializing in Information Security and public-private collaboration. Mr. Higgins. has held senior leadership positions at several of the world's most influential companies. Prior to CoreWeave, he served as the Director of Product Security at Google​ ​and as ​the CISO at Block, Inc. (formerly Square, Inc.) and Snap​, Inc. His expertise encompasses product security, offensive security, and cloud security, with a strong emphasis on emerging cyber threats and their impact on both private enterprises and Fortune 100 companies​.
