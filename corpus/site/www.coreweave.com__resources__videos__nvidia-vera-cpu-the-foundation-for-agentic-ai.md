Agentic AI demands high-performance CPUs. NVIDIA Vera is the CPU for agents, helping them call tools, execute code, and power reinforcement learning loops. CPU performance determines how quickly AI factories can turn intelligence into action. This session explores Vera’s agentic sandbox performance and its role in accelerating orchestration, data processing, and other CPU-intensive workloads that keep GPUs fed and agents responsive.
Speakers: Hannah Coutand, Director of Product Marketing, Vera CPU, NVIDIA
