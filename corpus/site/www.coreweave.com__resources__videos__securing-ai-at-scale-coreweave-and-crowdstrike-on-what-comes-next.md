Jim Higgins, CISO at CoreWeave, and Bartley Richardson, Chief AI & Autonomous Systems Officer at CrowdStrike, sit down for a candid conversation on what it takes to protect the infrastructure and workloads powering AI at scale. Find out how CoreWeave and CrowdStrike are thinking about what comes next, from building security into AI environments to exploring how AI can help defenders stay ahead of increasingly sophisticated threats. Get an inside look at the ideas, technologies, and emerging approaches that could shape the next generation of AI and cyber defense.
Speakers: Bartley Richardson, Chief of AI, CrowdStrike; Jim Higgins, Chief Information Security Officer, CoreWeave
