## Built for continuous AI improvement

See how CoreWeave Forge connects the tools and workflows behind AI development, so every trace, evaluation, and iteration helps make the next version better.

### Your best work goes into the agent, not wiring tools together

Training, tracking, evaluation, agent development, and serving have traditionally lived as separate products, with a different login, tab, and data handoff required for tool. Forge connects your whole loop under one account and makes it accessible via one navigation.

### Confidence is earned on your own workload

No team can watch every exchange an agent has in production. CoreWeave Forge shows you how it behaves before you ship and while it runs, so the first sign of trouble reaches you with time to act.

### Production behavior fuels what you build next

The traces you flag become the training data and the evaluations you gate releases on. The assets generated from your production runs stay portable and stay yours.

### Weights & Biases Models

Tracking, evaluation, and versioning stay wired to the work as it moves from experiment to production — the record travels with the work instead of living in a system you reconcile by hand.

### Serverless SFT

Supervised fine-tuning with nothing to provision — turn your curated datasets into a sharper model without managing training infrastructure.

### Serverless RL

Train agents against reward signals with nothing to provision. Built on ART, the Agent Reinforcement Trainer, with RULER providing automatic reward scoring — no hand-labeled data or hand-crafted reward functions required.

### Model Distillation

Carry what a larger model learned into a smaller, faster one — so quality you’ve already proven can ship at lower cost and latency.

### Tools

### CoreWeave ARIA

ARIA analyzes your experiment history, surfaces what drove a change, and proposes what’s worth trying next — your judgment stays in charge.

### CoreWeave Registry

Every model checkpoint and agent configuration versioned in open formats, so your next attempt starts from any point in your history, on any stack.

### CoreWeave Notebooks

Notebooks live in the environment your work runs in, so what you prototype carries straight into training, evaluation, and production.

### Inference

### Serverless Inference

Serve models from autoscaling endpoints with nothing to provision — pick from the Model Catalog and pay for what you use.

### Dedicated Inference

Serve your models on dedicated capacity reserved for you — predictable performance and isolation for production workloads that can’t share.

### Agent Development

### CoreWeave Agent Lens

See every step your agent took, score it against your own production traces, and catch drift while the fix is still a small one.

### CoreWeave Sandboxes

A clean, isolated environment for every run, so the safe place to test is there the moment you need it.

### Any model

Serve, evaluate, and improve the models you choose. Go as deep as the weights on your own model.

### Any framework

Keep the frameworks your teams already know and trust while Forge connects the work and context that moves between them.
