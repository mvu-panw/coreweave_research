- Run robot simulation, sim evaluation, synthetic data generation, and digital twins on bare-metal RTX PRO 6000 nodes built for CPU-heavy simulation loops
- Keep simulation and inference in the same cloud as your training jobs, with SUNK, CKS, CoreWeave AI Object Storage, and Weights & Biases already connected
- Work with CoreWeave engineers who have physical AI experience to close the sim-to-real gap and get to deployment faster
