HOW ARENA WORKS

### Evaluate real workloads with confidence

CoreWeave ARENA provides an end-to-end environment for evaluating real workloads on purpose-built, production-grade AI infrastructure and Forge, CoreWeave's connected developer platform. Evaluate new GPU generations, validate multi-node scaling, pressure-test scheduling and orchestration behavior, test Forge capabilities like evals, agents, and inference, and measure throughput and cost under real load.

### 1. Define objectives and success criteria

CoreWeave Direct-to-Expert support works with you to align on workload, prerequisites, and the success criteria you can use to evaluate outcomes.

### 2. Run guided workloads

Your workload runs against the configurations you defined, through guided workflows across Forge and purpose-built AI infrastructure.

### 3. Review the results

Get reproducible performance and cost results mapped to your success criteria, with clear insights into the drivers behind them.

### 4. Develop a next-step plan

A clear recommendation that allows your team to proceed through production, validate deeper, refactor, or pause with an evidence-backed path.

## Your path into ARENA

ARENA Pass gets you into a guided ARENA evaluation. You request one, get qualified, and get matched with a CoreWeave AI expert who scopes the run around your workload.
How it flows:
- Request your ARENA Pass
- Get qualified for the program
- Get matched with a CoreWeave AI expert
- Your expert scopes the evaluation to your workload, then your ARENA run begins
