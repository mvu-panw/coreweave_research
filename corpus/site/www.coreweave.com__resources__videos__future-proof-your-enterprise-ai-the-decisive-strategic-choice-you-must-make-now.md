## Episode 6: AI Cloud Essentials Podcast
AI-native cloud strategy is quickly becoming the difference between scaling AI and stalling out. As agentic AI moves from pilot to production, infrastructure decisions now shape cost control, speed to market, and long-term competitive advantage.
In this episode, host Ritu Jyoti sits down with Jean English, Chief Marketing Officer at CoreWeave, to explain why general-purpose clouds weren’t built for AI at scale—and how AI-native architectures unlock better economics, higher GPU utilization, and faster innovation.
You’ll learn how to assess whether your current AI stack is enabling growth or creating friction, how to move from prototype to production with confidence, and how to align AI infrastructure decisions with executive-level outcomes. If you want to future-proof your enterprise AI strategy, this conversation provides a clear path forward.
