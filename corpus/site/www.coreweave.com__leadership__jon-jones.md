# Jon Jones

Chief Revenue Officer

Chief Revenue Officer

Copied

Jon Jones is Chief Revenue Officer at CoreWeave, where he leads the company’s Global Field Organization and go-to-market strategy. He is responsible for scaling CoreWeave through its next phase of growth, including global expansion and industry verticalization, while driving enterprise adoption, deepening customer success and support, and diversifying the company’s global customer base.
Prior to CoreWeave, Jones served as global vice president of startups and venture capital at Amazon Web Services (AWS), where he played a key role in driving global cloud adoption and expanding AWS services from pre-product launch through large-scale growth, with a strong emphasis on AI innovation. A seasoned technology and AI executive, Jones brings more than two decades of Silicon Valley experience spanning startup founding, enterprise leadership, and guiding organizations through periods of significant business expansion and transformation.
He holds a B.A. in Economics from The University of California, Davis and an M.B.A from The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.
