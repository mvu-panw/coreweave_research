CoreWeave Fully Connected 2026 Day 2: CoreWeave CEO Mike Intrator takes the Hot Ones hot seat with Sean Evans, plus keynotes from Parag Agrawal, Dylan Patel, and Formula One World Champion and Aston Martin Aramco F1™ Team ambassador Jenson Button. Day 2 was about what happens after the launch: scaling infrastructure, cutting operational friction, and keeping teams sharp when the stakes are high. Parag Agrawal, founder and CEO of Parallel Web Systems, explains how agents are reshaping the web. Dylan Patel, founder of SemiAnalysis, breaks down cloud security across the full stack. Formula One World Champion Jenson Button talks about building team culture under pressure. Then Mike Intrator works through the wings while Sean Evans asks the questions.
Speakers: Brian Venturo, Chief Strategy Officer and Co-Founder, CoreWeave; Chen Goldberg, Executive Vice President, Product & Engineering, CoreWeave; Dylan Patel, Founder, SemiAnalysis; Mike Intrator, Chief Executive Officer, CoreWeave; Sean Evans, Host, Hot Ones; Jon Jones, Chief Revenue Officer, CoreWeave; Parag Agrawal, Founder, Parallel Web Systems; Jean English, Chief Marketing Officer, CoreWeave; Jenson Button, Team Ambassador, Aston Martin Aramco Formula One
