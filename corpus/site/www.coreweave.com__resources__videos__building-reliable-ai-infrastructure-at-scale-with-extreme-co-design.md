In this session, learn how NVIDIA and CoreWeave are applying extreme co-design across compute, networking, software, and operations to bring up next-generation AI factories built for the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform. We’ll explore how Vera CPUs, Rubin GPUs, NVLink, ConnectX, BlueField, and Spectrum-X Ethernet work together to deliver predictable performance, resiliency, and efficiency for model builders and AI-native companies serving production AI applications.
Speakers: Kirthi K. Devleker, Senior Product Marketing Manager, NVIDIA; Brian Sparks, Senior Director, Networking Product Marketing, NVIDIA
