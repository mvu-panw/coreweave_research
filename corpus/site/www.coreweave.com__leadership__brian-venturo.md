# Brian Venturo

Chief Strategy Officer, Co-founder

Chief Strategy Officer, Co-founder

Copied

Brian Venturo is co-founder and Chief Strategy Officer at CoreWeave, where he defines the company's platform vision, growth strategy, and position at the forefront of AI infrastructure.

Venturo spent CoreWeave's first seven years as Chief Technology Officer, building the technical foundation that powers the company's AI cloud platform today. His leadership helped establish CoreWeave's reputation for delivering infrastructure purpose-built for the unique requirements of AI.
Prior to CoreWeave, Venturo was a partner at Hudson Ridge Asset Management LLC and a portfolio manager at Natsource Asset Management LLC. 

He holds a B.A. in Economics from Haverford College.
