# Bring us your hardest physical AI problems
Tell us where your program is actually stuck: sim-to-real validation, data volumes your cloud was never built for, training runs that fail at 80%. Maybe you’re just looking for the opportunity to embed AI into how your engineers actually work? Let’s talk. We'll put you in front of the CoreWeave engineers who've solved problems like this before, whether that means testing your workload on our infrastructure or embedding in your team to build the solution together.
What happens after you submit 

- A working session, not a pitch. Connect with engineers who have delivered physical AI programs at automotive, robotics, and industrial scale, not an account team reading from a deck.
- A read on your actual workload. We’ll help you understand where your pipeline is losing GPU hours today, and what the same pipeline looks like on bare metal with storage that keeps pace.
- A path to proof, if infrastructure performance is the open question. We scope a CoreWeave ARENA run on your real pipeline, so your go/no-go rests on real-world production numbers, not generic benchmarks or reports.
- The right team for the job. If the gap is engineering depth, not infrastructure, we bring in Physical AI Field Engineering, CoreWeave experts who embed with your team and build the model with you.
