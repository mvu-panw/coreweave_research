SUNK Anywhere extends CoreWeave’s unified training system to cross-cloud and on-prem environments, giving you a faster and more secure path from POC to production.
No more losing time switching tools, workflows, or operating models every time the infrastructure changes. SUNK Anywhere lets you operate demanding AI workloads with consistent workflows and operational discipline across environments and clouds. That consistency helps platform teams expand without fragmentation, and researchers use familiar scheduling and workflows as their infrastructure footprint grows.
