We are excited to share that CoreWeave has been recognized as a Visionary in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cloud AI Infrastructure. Check back for a complimentary copy of the full report soon!
We believe that CoreWeave’s recognition as the highest in the Visionary quadrant is grounded in outcomes CoreWeave customers have achieved in production. Today, nine of the 10 leading foundation model providers rely on CoreWeave to stay at the frontier of AI. Mistral AI achieved 2.5x faster training on CoreWeave Cloud, compressing the cycle time between research hypothesis and result1. IBM delivered 80% faster model training for enterprise AI workloads2. CoreWeave was the first AI cloud to bring up and validate NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72, and — in collaboration with NVIDIA and IBM — submitted the largest-ever cluster to MLPerf Training v5.0, a cluster 34x larger than the next GB200 submission.
Gartner delivers actionable, objective insight to executives and their teams. Its expert guidance and tools enable faster, smarter decisions and stronger performance on an organization’s mission-critical priorities.
The Gartner Magic Quadrant evaluates vendors based on their Ability to Execute and Completeness of Vision. We are honored to be included among the recognized vendors in this important report. Learn more about the Magic Quadrant.
