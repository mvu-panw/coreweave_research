Most AI teams operating at scale run across multiple clouds. Their stack, assembled over the years from many vendors for everything from training and inference to security and storage, grows harder to manage with every new project. Each time a team expands onto a new cloud, connecting that stack to new infrastructure becomes the slowest and riskiest part of the move, and the platform team is left to verify that every integration actually works. Until now, the partner collaborations that could ease that work ran one relationship at a time, largely invisible to customers.
Today, we're introducing the CoreWeave Partner Network: a curated network of technology partners offering integrations that are validated on CoreWeave under real-world conditions. It brings together the valuable relationships CoreWeave has built over years—with CrowdStrike, VAST Data, Reflection, IBM, and many more—into one place, so your team can see exactly which tools are proven and ready to run on CoreWeave Cloud and rapidly adopt them with confidence.

## How  the CoreWeave Partner Network works
The CoreWeave Partner Network is a proven network of technology partners spanning four segments that together cover the full AI stack:
- Infrastructure:  Hardware and systems partners covering compute, storage, networking, servers, and interconnects, including VAST Data, Dell, AMD, IBM, and Pulumi
- Data services:  Partners that improve how AI data is stored, processed, moved, and accessed across the AI lifecycle, including ClickHouse, Encord, LanceDB, and Datology
- ISVs:  Software partners—including Grafana, Datadog, Union, SkyPilot, IBM, Pinecone, Redis, LlamaIndex, and Clockwork—spanning orchestration, observability, and model development, with a dedicated security segment anchored by CrowdStrike, Zscaler, and Cranium  
- Models and inference:  Partners that provide the foundational software and services used to build, train, serve, and scale AI applications, including Reflection, RadixArk, Inferact, OpenRouter, and Red Hat
Together, they support every stage of the AI loop, from running and observing models in production to curating data, improving, and evaluating what ships next.
Every partner in the CoreWeave Partner Network meets the same bar: a working integration, tested and proven on CoreWeave infrastructure under real-world production conditions. Partners join based on real demand; they're in the network because our customers already run them or are asking for them.

## Partner technology proven at production scale
Customers are already building on CoreWeave with partner technology throughout their stack:
- IBM trained its Granite models on CoreWeave, built around one of the first instances in the world to stand up NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 clusters
- Cohere built North, its agentic AI platform, on one of the industry's first production NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 clusters, with 3x faster training compared to NVIDIA  Hopper
- Mistral AI trains its models entirely on CoreWeave, citing 2.5x faster training on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72s
- Decart runs NVIDIA HGX B200 at production scale through its collaboration with CoreWeave, whose platform includes Weights & Biases.

## What this means for your team

### Bring the stack you already trust
The tools your team runs today—serving layers, observability dashboards, security tooling, and more—are already integrated on CoreWeave Cloud. Expanding onto CoreWeave for scale doesn't mean rebuilding what already works. Inference partners keep your models serving without a rebuild; Grafana and Datadog carry over the observability you've built runbooks around; and CrowdStrike, Zscaler, and Cranium anchor a security segment so your existing threat visibility and response tooling comes with you.

### Start from integrations that already work
Integrations that pass on a spec sheet can still fail under real traffic. Every integration in the CoreWeave Partner Network is tested directly on CoreWeave infrastructure under production conditions before it reaches you, so the validation work is done before you ever touch it.

### Skip the sorting work
A marketplace with thousands of listings hands the vetting over to your team. Asking an LLM gets you the market's consensus or  what's popular, not what's proven. The CoreWeave Partner Network is deliberately curated: select partners join when customers are already running them or asking for them, and every segment is held to the same standard. The result is a curated, vetted list that still spans every layer of your stack.

## Build on anything, locked-in to nothing
The CoreWeave Partner Network will continue to expand. The same demand-led model that brought infrastructure, data, and security partners into the network is extending to the tools your agents call, too, starting with search. Exa, Parallel Web Systems, and You.com join as partners to give agents built on CoreWeave a shared search layer, reachable through one integration instead of a separate contract for each.
Most closed models come with web search built in. Most open-weight models don't, so teams choosing open weights for cost, control, or customization have had to bolt search on themselves, one provider integration at a time. 
That's the gap this closes. Turn it on with a single setting on a standard inference request, or reach it the same way from inside a CoreWeave Sandbox; no separate SDK, no separate account to stand up. And, because this isn't an exclusive arrangement, choosing this integration today doesn't lock in what's available tomorrow. We're announcing this at Fully Connected, with the rest of the integration following in the weeks after.
This collaboration follows the same principle behind everything else in the CoreWeave Partner Network: proven solutions, not a list assembled for an announcement. As these integrations come online, they'll be held to the same bar as every partner here today.
