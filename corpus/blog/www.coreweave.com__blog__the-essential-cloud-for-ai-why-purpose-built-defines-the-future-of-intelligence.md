## From general-purpose to purpose-built: The new foundation for AI
‍The first generation of cloud closed the gap between compute and accessibility. General-purpose clouds made it possible for anyone to build and scale an idea from a laptop to the world. They built the building blocks that every modern digital business still relies on. For years, that model worked beautifully. But when AI arrived, we all tried to stretch those same systems to meet a completely different kind of demand built around GPUs, massive parallelism, and constant data movement. That’s when things started to break.
Drawing on our experience running some of the largest GPU clusters for the most demanding organizations, we realized a new approach was needed. Not a patch or a retrofit, but a foundation built specifically for AI, one that could handle the scale, complexity, and speed this new era demands. That foundation became CoreWeave Cloud where every part of the stack has to work together: the infrastructure, the data, the orchestration, tools and the people who understand how to run it all.

### The CoreWeave Cloud platform: #1 AI Cloud
CoreWeave Cloud is purpose-built for the AI era, delivering performance through a vertically-integrated platform that unites compute, storage, orchestration, and intelligence into one optimized system. Its foundational infrastructure combines high-bandwidth, low-latency networking (InfiniBand, RoCE, and proprietary interconnects) with the latest GPUs delivering microsecond-latency performance recognized by SemiAnalysis’s only Platinum ClusterMAX rating.
Data and storage technologies like CoreWeave AI Object Storage and Local Object Transport Accelerator (LOTA) feed GPUs at up to 7 GiB/s per GPU, scaling linearly and enabling continuous model training without egress fees. Infrastructure Control brings together Kubernetes and SLURM, with CoreWeave’s implementation, SUNK (SLURM on Kubernetes), unifying both environments. This approach gives teams flexible scheduling, rapid spin-up times, and real-time visibility across diverse AI workloads.
To serve AI developers working on the latest agentic applications, AIOps and Agent Development integrate Weights & Biases and OpenPipe to accelerate experimentation, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning at scale, bridging model development and production deployment seamlessly. Mission Control connects it all, providing end-to-end visibility, governance, and orchestration across the stack, ensuring every layer operates in harmony to deliver unmatched reliability, transparency, and speed for the world’s most ambitious AI workloads
