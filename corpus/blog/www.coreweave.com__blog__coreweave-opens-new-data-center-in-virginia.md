We are thrilled to announce the launch of our new data center, US-EAST-04, equipped with some of the latest NVIDIA accelerated computing offerings. This state-of-the-art, 24.6+ MW facility became operational in early August 2024, allowing us to continue to lead in providing cutting-edge infrastructure.
What this means for CoreWeave and our customers
This expansion significantly enhances our computational capabilities, providing robust support for our growing suite of services and products. For our customers, the increased capacity means faster innovation with access to more computing power and a wider range of NVIDIA accelerated computing offerings to fit their inference, batch processing, and training needs. New resources  available for consumption at US-EAST-04 include:
- NVIDIA GPUs and Superchip: NVIDIA  L40 GPU, NVIDIA L40S GPU, NVIDIA A100 Tensor Core GPU, NVIDIA GH200 Grace Hopper Superchip, NVIDIA HGX H100
- CPUs: AMD Genoa High Core, AMD  Genoa High Performance
- Storage: Premium NVMe File System
“As our company continues to grow, we are not only growing the single tenant portion of our business but also exponentially growing the infrastructure behind our core multi-tenant cloud. We’re equipping ourselves to take on a greater number of larger, more diverse workloads.” – Brian Venturo, CSO at CoreWeave.
Setting a new standard for data center deployment
Our commitment to innovation and growth is unwavering. The fast-changing AI market demands a redesign of the data center to stay on the bleeding edge and deliver the most performant infrastructure to our customers. This redesign incorporates evolutions at the hardware level and beyond: state-of-the-art architecture, cooling systems, and software all come together to handle the higher power density, heat, and processing needs of AI.
In the past year, we have seen a significant evolution in our data centers, such as increasing rack power density from 16 kW per cabinet to over 100 kW per cabinet in just 12 months. This leap in capacity highlights the necessity of designing our facilities for today's requirements and building the capacity for our customers to continue scaling.
 “CoreWeave is all about challenging ourselves and continuously improving what we deliver to our customers. That requires diligence, accountability, and teamwork so that we can continue to raise the bar.” — Mike Terlizzi, SVP of Data Center Operations at CoreWeave.
US-EAST-04 is fully integrated with the CoreWeave proprietary software stack, ensuring safety, reliability, and peak performance for all customers accessing Compute. CoreWeave adheres to industry best practices and standards, prioritizing the security and privacy of every customer’s business-critical workloads.
We are excited to bring this new facility online and look forward to the continued innovation and growth it will bring to our customers. For more information, get in touch with our sales team here.
